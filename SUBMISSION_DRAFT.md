# CreditSeal

**Draft status — READY for owner review.** All three fixture outcomes finalized and were verified through public reads. The updated reviewer evidence awaits publication approval.

**Category** Standalone Intelligent Contract

**One-liner** Verify public asset attribution on-chain against an immutable policy version.

**Description**

CreditSeal gives registries and creator tools a shared way to verify credit in public HTML. A wallet registers an exact asset reference and appends policies that specify a creator name and credit link. For each assessment, GenLayer validators fetch a supported page independently and agree on the facts that determine PASS, FAIL, or UNVERIFIABLE. Builders can read the governing policy and immutable assessment through public contract methods. V1 supports commit-pinned static HTML figures and evaluates published caption evidence. Copyright, identity, and licensing are outside its scope.
